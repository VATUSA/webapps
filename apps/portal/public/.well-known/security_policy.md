# Security Policy — VATUSA (VATSIM US Division)

## Scope

This policy applies to all web properties and services operated by VATUSA, including but not limited to vatusa.net and associated subdomains.

## Authorized Testing

**Security testing, penetration testing, and vulnerability research against VATUSA systems is not permitted without explicit prior written authorization.** Unauthorized testing — regardless of intent — may violate applicable law. If you are interested in conducting authorized security research, please reach out to us at `vatusa6@vatusa.net` before beginning any testing activity.

## Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in one of our systems in the course of normal use, we ask that you:

1. **Do not exploit it.** Refrain from accessing, modifying, or deleting data beyond what is necessary to confirm the vulnerability exists.
2. **Report it promptly.** Send a description of the issue to `vatusa6@vatusa.net` as soon as reasonably possible.
3. **Give us time to respond.** We ask for a reasonable period to investigate and remediate before any public disclosure.
4. **Act in good faith.** We will do the same in return.

We appreciate responsible disclosure and will make every effort to acknowledge your report, keep you informed of our progress, and address confirmed vulnerabilities in a timely manner.

## Out of Scope

The following are considered out of scope and should not be reported:

- Vulnerabilities in third-party services or software not under VATUSA control
- Issues that require physical access to VATUSA infrastructure
- Social engineering or phishing attacks targeting VATUSA staff or members
- Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks of any kind

## Contact

To report a vulnerability or inquire about authorized research, please contact:

**Email:** vatusa6@vatusa.net

We are a volunteer-run organization. While we cannot offer monetary compensation for reports, we are genuinely grateful for good-faith efforts to help keep our community secure.
